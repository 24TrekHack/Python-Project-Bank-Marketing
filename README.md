Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

Project Definition

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on bank marketing data to understand the results of telephone marketing campaigns and factors related to whether customers subscribe to a term deposit.

Dataset Use Case

The dataset contains 41,188 rows and 21 columns. It includes customer details, loan information, campaign contact details, previous campaign results, economic indicators, and the target column y.

The dataset can be used to compare subscription results across customer groups and campaign details. In the dataset, 4,640 customers subscribed and 36,548 customers did not subscribe.


Visualizations

The following visualizations will be created:

Subscription Outcome Bar Chart – To compare the number of customers who subscribed and did not subscribe. There are 4,640 yes outcomes and 36,548 no outcomes.

Customer Age Histogram – To understand the distribution of customer ages. Ages range from 17 to 98 years, with an average of about 40 years.

Subscription Rate by Job Bar Chart – To compare subscription rates across job categories. The rate is about 31.43% for students, 25.23% for retired customers, and 6.89% for blue-collar customers.

Contacts by Month Bar Chart – To compare the number of campaign records across months. May has the highest number, with 13,769 records.

Subscription Rate by Contact Method Bar Chart – To compare subscription rates for cellular and telephone contacts. The rate is about 14.74% for cellular contacts and 5.23% for telephone contacts.

Subscription Rate by Previous Campaign Outcome Bar Chart – To compare current subscription rates based on previous campaign results. The rate is about 65.11% when the previous outcome was success, 14.23% for failure, and 8.83% when no previous outcome was recorded.

Call Duration Box Plot – To compare call durations for customers who subscribed and those who did not. The average duration is about 553 seconds for yes outcomes and 221 seconds for no outcomes.

Subscription Rate by Number of Campaign Contacts Bar Chart – To see how subscription rates vary with the number of contacts. The rate is about 13.04% for one contact and 3.11% for 11 or more contacts.
